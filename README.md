## 👨‍💻 About Me

> 🇮🇩 *Prefer Indonesian? [Baca versi Bahasa Indonesia](./README.id.md)*

I am a **Backend & Network Engineer** based in **Tuban, Indonesia**, bridging the gap between high-level application development and low-level network operations. I specialize in building robust web applications, designing scalable backend systems, and securing Linux infrastructures.

I am passionate about engineering practical solutions that solve real-world problems. My expertise spans across the entire stack—from crafting RESTful APIs and managing databases to configuring firewalls, analyzing packets, and deploying automated network infrastructures.

**🎯 Core Competencies:**
*   **Backend Engineering:** Developing scalable APIs, microservices, and business logic.
*   **Network & Security:** Firewall configuration, DNS filtering, packet analysis, and secure routing.
*   **Infrastructure & DevOps:** Linux system administration, containerization (Docker), and automation scripting.
*   **Full-Stack Delivery:** Integrating seamless frontend experiences with powerful backend architectures.

Currently, I am expanding my expertise in **Network Security, Infrastructure Automation, and Cloud/On-Premise Deployments** through hands-on enterprise projects and self-hosted environments.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

*Instead of just writing code, I build systems that deliver impact.*

#### 🧠 [MahasigMind](https://github.com/RusdiEneri/MahasigMind) | *Mental Health & Wellness Platform*
> A comprehensive web application for emotional wellness tracking, reflective journaling, and seamless psychologist consultation bookings.
*   **Tech:** `Laravel 11`, `React 19`, `TypeScript`, `Inertia.js`, `Tailwind CSS`
*   **Impact:** Delivered a highly responsive full-stack SPA experience, ensuring secure handling of sensitive user health data and smooth real-time UI updates.

#### 🛒 [Cuanin](https://github.com/RusdiEneri/Cuanin) | *Preloved E-Commerce Marketplace*
> A dynamic multi-vendor marketplace featuring role-based access control, wishlists, and integrated WhatsApp price negotiation.
*   **Tech:** `Laravel 12`, `Tailwind CSS`, `Vite`, `MySQL`
*   **Impact:** Engineered a scalable database schema to handle complex cart/checkout flows and integrated third-party messaging APIs for seamless buyer-seller communication.

#### 🚚 [J&T Express Scheduling](https://github.com/RusdiEneri/jt-express-scheduling) | *Algorithmic Shift Optimizer*
> An automated employee shift scheduling engine utilizing a **Genetic Algorithm** to resolve complex hard and soft logistical constraints.
*   **Tech:** `Python`, `Streamlit`, `Pandas`, `OpenPyXL`
*   **Impact:** Reduced manual scheduling hours by processing large datasets and generating optimal shift matrices based on custom operational rules.

#### 🏢 [UD. Alam Makmur Jaya](https://github.com/RusdiEneri/alam-makmur-jaya) | *Business Information System*
> A centralized web-based ERP system for managing daily sales transactions, inventory, staff payroll, and financial reporting.
*   **Tech:** `Express.js`, `Vite`, `Vanilla JS`, `REST API`
*   **Impact:** Digitalized traditional business operations, providing stakeholders with real-time analytics and automated PDF reporting.
