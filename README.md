- **Tech Stack:** `Laravel 11` · `React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Inertia.js` · `Tailwind CSS`
- **Key Architecture:** Engineered a responsive SPA architecture, ensuring strict data privacy for sensitive health records and real-time state synchronization.

#### 🛒 [Cuanin](https://github.com/RusdiEneri/Cuanin) · *Preloved E-Commerce Marketplace*
> Dynamic multi-vendor marketplace featuring role-based access control, automated wishlists, and direct WhatsApp price negotiation.
- **Tech Stack:** `Laravel 12` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Vite` · `MySQL`
- **Key Architecture:** Structured relational schemas to handle concurrent cart/checkout workflows with third-party messaging integrations.

> Automated workforce scheduling engine utilizing a **Genetic Algorithm** to resolve complex hard and soft logistical shift constraints.
- **Tech Stack:** `Python` · `Streamlit` · `Pandas` · `OpenPyXL`
- **Key Architecture:** Cut down hours of manual scheduling by executing heuristics over multidimensional constraint matrices.

#### 🏢 [UD. Alam Makmur Jaya](https://github.com/RusdiEneri/alam-makmur-jaya) · *Business ERP & Information System*
> Centralized management system for inventory replenishment, point-of-sale transactions, payroll, and financial bookkeeping.
- **Tech Stack:** `Express.js` · `Vite` · `Vanilla JS` · `REST API`
- **Key Architecture:** Digitalized traditional retail operations with real-time analytics dashboards and automated transactional PDF reporting.
